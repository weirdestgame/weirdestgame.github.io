A little about us

Weird ideas are good ideas.

Weirdest Ideas is an independent game studio with a soft spot for playful concepts, inviting design, and the little moments that make people smile.

We turn “what if?” into something you can play.

Our work starts with curiosity. We explore unusual concepts, shape them into approachable experiences, and keep the focus on making every session feel rewarding.

From the first sketch to the final tap, we believe a game can be simple to enjoy and still full of personality. That is the spirit behind every idea we bring to life.
